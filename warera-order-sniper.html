Extra decimal places beyond the rival's price. Four turns 0.0001 into 0.00010001.

If the browser can't reach the API, open the endpoint in a tab and paste the JSON here.

Prices are read as text straight from the response and handled with exact integer arithmetic, so a price like 0.100000001 is never flattened by JavaScript's floating point. What you see is what the server sent.

Beating works from the rival's own precision. Against 0.0001 with four extra places you get 0.00010001 — a step of 0.00000001 rather than the far more expensive jump to 0.00011.
