How this is measuredread once, then ignore
Everything here comes from the collector, which reads the game with a key that never leaves its workflow and republishes the result every quarter of an hour. The browser cannot page trades itself: transaction.getPaginatedTransactions answers an unauthenticated request with a 401. Each fill names both sides, so a whale is measured rather than guessed: accounts are ranked by the gold they moved across every good, and only those with at least three trades or two different goods qualify, so the one-off counterparty of a large fill is not mistaken for one. Books are capped at 100 orders a side by the game, so a deeper queue reads as exactly 100 and the page says when a side is at that cap. Momentum compares the last hour against the good's own average over the history held, so it says "unusual for this good" rather than "busy". Nothing is uploaded and no key is needed.
