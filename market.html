How this is measuredread once, then ignore
Trades come from transaction.getPaginatedTransactions filtered to transactionType: "trading", which is how goods actually fill. Every row names both sides, so a whale is measured rather than guessed: accounts are ranked by gold moved across all goods in the window, and only those with at least three trades or two different goods qualify, so the one-off counterparty of a large fill is not mistaken for one. Books come from tradingOrder.getTopOrders, capped at 100 orders a side by the API, so a deeper queue than that reads as exactly 100. Momentum compares the last hour against the window's own average, so it says "unusual for this good" rather than "busy". Everything is held in your browser; nothing is uploaded.
