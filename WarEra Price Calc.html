Prices step in units of 0.001, so an exact match isn't always possible. The three ticks let you choose which side of your target to land on — one under is the one to take when you're undercutting a rival listing.

The maths: figure = target ÷ 1.01, rounded to three decimals. Dividing is not the same as taking 1% off — at 100 the two answers differ by a full cent.

Change the tax field if your country or item type is charged a different rate. It's remembered next time you open this page.
